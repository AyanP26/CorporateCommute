# CorporateCommute

CorporateCommute is a cross-platform carpooling mobile app we built to help suburban coworkers and daily commuters coordinate shared rides to and from regional office parks. In suburban areas surrounding major commercial hubs, public transit lines like commuter rail or express bus systems rarely reach spread-out business complexes, leaving thousands of workers with no choice but to drive alone. Every weekday morning, commuters living in adjacent residential neighborhoods take the exact same twenty to thirty-mile highway routes, burning through expensive fuel, paying toll fees, and sitting in bumper-to-bumper traffic before competing for parking spaces in crowded office garages. We developed CorporateCommute to replace those stressful solo drives with organized, reliable coworker carpools. Commuters register with their corporate email accounts to create a verified, trusted environment where employees feel secure riding together. Through our app, drivers can post their upcoming commute by entering their neighborhood origin, workplace destination, departure time, vehicle make and model, and available passenger seats. Coworkers heading to the same office campus can browse active rides on an interactive map, filter listings by morning arrival or evening departure times, and send a seat request directly to the driver. Drivers receive instant notifications where they can review pending requests, check the rider's company details, and accept or decline with a single tap, which immediately locks in the reservation and updates remaining seats in real time across both iOS and Android. To keep pickup logistics smooth without requiring anyone to give out their personal phone number, each confirmed carpool opens a dedicated in-app group chat where drivers and riders can coordinate exact curbside pickup spots, adjust timing by a few minutes, or confirm building drop-offs. Furthermore, our built-in 24-hour departure lock prevents drivers or riders from canceling at the last second, ensuring nobody gets stranded right before their morning shift. A dedicated "My Carpools" tab organizes every ride into distinct host and passenger sections, letting commuters monitor their upcoming weekly schedules and departure times at a glance. By turning three or four separate cars into a single shared ride, our app helps coworkers save upwards of $150 a month on gasoline and tolls, eliminates parking lot congestion at suburban business parks, and makes the daily drive to work far more manageable.
